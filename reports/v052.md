# Hyper-encoder embedding probe — v0.52 (untied, no-residual)

Single-checkpoint report (`v052`). Analysis runs on the **final vector the model uses** (residual **off**: `E = encoder_out`). Bit-exact encoder re-implementation (max|Δ|=0 vs `model.py`). Hyper-tokens are real English n-grams (K=2/3/4). A shared *ruler* vector is not guaranteed (see ②), so ④ and ⑤ are shown **both** on the raw vector and after ruler removal. Method: see `README.md`.

## Reading

- **output**: **prefix-sensitive (reads the head)** (change-first/change-last = 3.31× at K=4). It also **collapses onto a shared ruler** (cos-with-mean 0.9987, 100% of length), well above the ordinary-token floor (0.2688) and random-init (0.627) — a learned, per-merge-uninformative direction.
- **input**: **tail-weighted** (change-first/change-last = 0.33× at K=4). Shared-ruler component is moderate (cos-with-mean 0.6899).
